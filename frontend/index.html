About FresherRadar

Finding a first job shouldn't mean checking twenty websites every morning.

01

Aggregate

We collect fresher jobs and internships from multiple public sources, so you see them in one place.

02

De-duplicate

The same opening is often posted on several sites. We detect those repeats and show each job once.

03

Classify

An LLM sorts every listing by role and city, which is what makes the filters reliable.

04

Stay fresh

The database is designed to refresh every 24 hours. The indicator in the top bar shows the last update.
